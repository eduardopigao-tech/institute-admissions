About Our Institution

A Brief History

The Institute of Law has been training legal professionals for generations. What started as a small group of legal scholars meeting to discuss jurisprudence has grown into one of the country's most respected law schools. Our graduates work as judges, prosecutors, defense attorneys, and legal advisors across the nation.

Over the years, we've stayed committed to the basics: solid legal training, an understanding of constitutional principles, and the kind of practical experience that matters when you're in a courtroom. Our faculty are practicing lawyers and legal professionals who bring real cases and real problems into the classroom. That's how we teach—not from textbooks alone, but from actual experience.

The entrance examination isn't just a filter. It's a reflection of what we believe law school should be. We're looking for students who are serious about the profession, who understand that being a lawyer is a responsibility, and who will contribute something meaningful to the legal system. That's why we maintain high standards. Our bar passage rates speak for themselves.

This August 2026, we're welcoming a new class of future lawyers. If you've made it this far in the admission process, you're already part of something bigger. Whether you pass on your first try or come back later, you're joining a community that values integrity, hard work, and service to the law.
